### T11 - Hidden Room Enumeration And Brute Force

STRIDE Category: Information Disclosure

Component or Asset: Room Search & Public Details API

Identified Threat: attacker uses automated scripts to brute-force sequential room IDs or query parameters to discover unlisted, blocked, or hidden rooms.

Possible Impact: unauthorized discovery of non-public hotel rooms, maintenance schedules, and unlisted rate structures.

Related Abuse Cases: AC11
