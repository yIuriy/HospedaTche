### AC01 - Room Availability Manipulation

Actor: malicious Receptionist or Manager.

Goal: manipulate room availability and affect booking decisions.

Conditions: staff member has valid access to room availability or blocking features.

Abuse flow:
1. Malicious staff member logs in with valid credentials.
2. Staff member opens room availability or blocking controls.
3. Staff member removes legitimate maintenance blocks or creates false availability blocks.
4. Guests and staff see incorrect room availability during search or reservation creation.

Impact: double booking, lost reservations, reservation data inconsistency, and hotel operation disruption.

Related STRIDE categories: Tampering, Denial of Service
