### T13 - Guest Privilege Escalation To Receptionist Chat Queue

STRIDE Category: Elevation of Privilege

Component or Asset: Staff Chat Queue & Receptionist Portal Interface

Identified Threat: guest tampers with authorization tokens or role parameters to access the receptionist chat queue interface and staff controls.

Possible Impact: guest gains staff-level access over all active hotel chats, reads confidential guest communications, and responds as hotel staff.

Related Abuse Cases: AC13
