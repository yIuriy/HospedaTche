### T07 - Payment Confirmation Blocking

STRIDE Category: Denial of Service

Component or Asset: Payment Status Synchronization

Identified Threat: an attacker prevents the system from receiving or processing the successful payment confirmation of another Guest's booking after the payment has already been completed.

Possible Impact: valid payments are not recognized, bookings are cancelled incorrectly, refund disputes may occur, and customer trust is reduced.

Related Abuse Cases: AC05
