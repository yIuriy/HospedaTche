### AC09 - Companion Registration Tampering

Actor: malicious Receptionist or Manager.

Goal: change companion data linked to a stay.

Conditions: staff member has valid access to companion registration features.

Abuse flow:
1. Malicious staff member logs in with valid credentials.
2. Staff member opens companion registration for a reservation.
3. Staff member changes companion names or count incorrectly.
4. Stay records show companion data that does not match hotel policy or real occupancy.

Impact: occupancy policy violation, incorrect stay records, guest disputes, and unreliable operational data.

Related STRIDE categories: Tampering
