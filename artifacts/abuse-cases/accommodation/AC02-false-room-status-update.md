### AC02 - False Room Status Update

Actor: malicious Receptionist or Manager.

Goal: falsify room status to hide occupancy, cleaning needs, or maintenance problems.

Conditions: staff member has valid access to room status update features.

Abuse flow:
1. Malicious staff member logs in with valid credentials.
2. Staff member opens the room status page.
3. Staff member changes a room status to Free, Occupied, Waiting Cleaning, Under Maintenance, or Reserved incorrectly.
4. Staff uses the false status during check-in, check-out, cleaning, or maintenance operations.

Impact: unsafe room assignment, delayed cleaning, hidden maintenance issues, and disrupted stay operations.

Related STRIDE categories: Tampering, Denial of Service
