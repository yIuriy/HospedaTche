### T10 - Permanent Guest Review Purge

STRIDE Category: Repudiation

Component or Asset: Guest Review Records & Moderation System

Identified Threat: attacker or rogue manager bypasses the "hide review" policy to permanently purge guest reviews from the database instead of keeping them hidden.

Possible Impact: permanent loss of historical guest feedback, inability to verify past review moderation decisions, and violation of review retention rules.

Related Abuse Cases: AC10
