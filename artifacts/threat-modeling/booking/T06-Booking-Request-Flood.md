### T06 - Booking Request Flood

STRIDE Category: Denial of Service

Component or Asset: Booking Availability

Identified Threat: an attacker submits a large number of booking requests without intending to complete the corresponding payments, temporarily occupying room availability and preventing legitimate Guests from creating new bookings.

Possible Impact: legitimate Guests are unable to book available rooms, booking availability becomes artificially exhausted, hotel revenue decreases, and system resources are unnecessarily consumed.
