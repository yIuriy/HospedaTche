### T12 - Misdirected Chat Billing Request And Staff Message Spoofing

STRIDE Category: Spoofing

Component or Asset: Guest Reception Chat Service & Staff Queue

Identified Threat: attacker or staff member spoofs receptionist messages or misdirects billing requests to the wrong guest chat session.

Possible Impact: unauthorized payment requests sent to wrong guests, guest financial loss, and breakdown of trust in receptionist communications.

Related Abuse Cases: AC12
