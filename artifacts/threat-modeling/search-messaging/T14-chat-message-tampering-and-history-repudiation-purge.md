### T14 - Chat Message Tampering And History Repudiation Purge

STRIDE Category: Repudiation

Component or Asset: Chat Database & Messaging Log Records

Identified Threat: user or staff member alters sent chat messages or purges chat history to destroy evidence of fraud or misconduct and deny past actions.

Possible Impact: inability to verify past chat communications during disputes, loss of audit evidence, and non-repudiation failure.

Related Abuse Cases: AC14
