### AC05 - Legitimate Receptionist Inactivation

Actor: malicious Manager.

Goal: inactivate legitimate Receptionist accounts.

Conditions: Manager has valid access to the receptionist management module.

Abuse flow:
1. Malicious Manager logs in with valid credentials.
2. Manager opens the receptionist management module.
3. Manager inactivates legitimate Receptionist accounts.
4. Receptionists lose access to the system and hotel operation is disrupted.

Impact: hotel operation disruption, blocked receptionist access, and delayed guest service.

Related STRIDE categories: Denial of Service, Tampering
