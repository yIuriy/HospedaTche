### T23 - Internal Staff Hierarchy Bypass

STRIDE Category: Elevation of Privilege

Component or Asset: Staff account management and inactivation API

Identified Threat: manager or receptionist user executes management or inactivation actions against peer or higher-level staff accounts due to missing target role hierarchy validation.

Possible Impact: unauthorized lockout of administrative accounts, disruption of hotel operational management, and privilege boundary violation.

Related Abuse Cases: AC21
