### AC11 - Maintenance Note Tampering

Actor: malicious Receptionist or Manager.

Goal: hide or alter maintenance information for a room.

Conditions: staff member has valid access to maintenance tracking features.

Abuse flow:
1. Malicious staff member logs in with valid credentials.
2. Staff member opens maintenance tracking for a room.
3. Staff member changes the maintenance note or removes the maintenance status incorrectly.
4. Room appears operational even though maintenance work is still needed.

Impact: hidden maintenance issues, unsafe room assignment, delayed repair, and loss of operational traceability.

Related STRIDE categories: Tampering, Denial of Service
