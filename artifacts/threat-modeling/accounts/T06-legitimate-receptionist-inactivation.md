### T06 - Legitimate Receptionist Inactivation

STRIDE Category: Denial of Service

Component or Asset: Receptionist management module

Identified Threat: malicious Manager with legitimate system access inactivates legitimate Receptionist accounts and disrupts hotel operation.

Possible Impact: receptionists lose access to the system, check-in and check-out operations are delayed, and hotel operation is disrupted.

Related Abuse Cases: AC05
