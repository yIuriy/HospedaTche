### AC23 - Legitimate Employee Inactivation

Actor: malicious user.

Goal: inactivate legitimate employee accounts.

Conditions: attacker has obtained access to the Administrator account.

Abuse flow:
1. Attacker logs in using the stolen Administrator account.
2. Attacker opens the employee management page.
3. Attacker inactivates legitimate employee accounts.
4. Legitimate employees lose access to the system and hotel operation is disrupted.

Impact: hotel operation interruption, blocked staff access, and service disruption.

Related STRIDE categories: Tampering
