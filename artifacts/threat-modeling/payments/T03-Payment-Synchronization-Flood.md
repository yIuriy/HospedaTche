### T03 - Payment Synchronization Flood

STRIDE Category: Denial of Service

Component or Asset: Payment Status Synchronization

Identified Threat: an attacker floods the payment status synchronization process with a large number of fake or repeated requests, preventing legitimate payment confirmations from being processed in a timely manner.

Possible Impact: delayed payment recognition, booking confirmation failures, increased server load, and service degradation.
