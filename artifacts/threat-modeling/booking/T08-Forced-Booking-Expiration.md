### T08 - Forced Booking Expiration

STRIDE Category: Denial of Service

Component or Asset: Booking Status

Identified Threat: an attacker forces the immediate expiration of pending bookings, preventing legitimate Guests from completing the payment within the allowed time.

Possible Impact: unintended booking cancellations, loss of revenue, customer dissatisfaction, and disruption of the booking process.
