### AC02 - Account Takeover

Actor: malicious user.

Goal: access another user's account and act as that user.

Conditions: attacker has stolen, guessed, or reused valid account credentials.

Abuse flow:
1. Attacker obtains valid email and password from a leak, phishing, or reuse.
2. Attacker logs in as the victim.
3. Attacker views private data or reservation history.
4. Attacker changes profile data, starts chat, or attempts booking and cancellation actions.

Impact: privacy exposure, unauthorized actions, financial dispute, and account trust loss.

Related STRIDE categories: Spoofing, Information Disclosure
