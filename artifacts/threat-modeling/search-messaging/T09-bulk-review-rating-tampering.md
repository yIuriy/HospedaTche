### T09 - Bulk Review Rating Tampering

STRIDE Category: Tampering

Component or Asset: Review Database & Hotel Reputation Rating System

Identified Threat: malicious employee or attacker with compromised credentials alters or overwrites guest reviews in bulk to display a false hotel rating status.

Possible Impact: severe reputational damage, financial loss, misleading prospective guests, and distortion of historical audit records.

Related Abuse Cases: AC09
